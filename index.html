Machine Learning × Mathematics

PDEs for ML

Learning physical laws with neural networks.

Partial Differential Equation
$$ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + \mathcal{N}[u] = f(x,t) $$
Neural Approximation
$$ u(x,t) \approx u_\theta(x,t) = \mathrm{NN}_\theta(x,t) $$
Physics-Informed Neural Network
$$ \boxed{ \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \lambda_f \mathcal{L}_{PDE} + \lambda_b \mathcal{L}_{BC} + \lambda_i \mathcal{L}_{IC} } $$
Physics Residual
$$ r_\theta(x,t) = \frac{\partial u_\theta}{\partial t} + \mathcal{N}[u_\theta] - f(x,t) $$
$\mathcal{L}_{PDE}$

Forces the neural network to satisfy the governing differential equation.

$\mathcal{L}_{BC}$

Enforces boundary conditions imposed by the physical system.

$\mathcal{L}_{IC}$

Enforces the initial state of the physical system.
